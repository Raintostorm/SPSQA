# Mythic recipes

Exactly three named board allies. At least one Epic or Legendary; Mythic ingredients are excluded. Two identical allies are allowed; three identical allies are forbidden. Minimum rarity cost: Epic + Common + Common. Maximum: Legendary + Legendary + Legendary, with at least two distinct identities.

- mythic_guardian: human_epic + human_common + human_common
- mythic_undead: dead_legendary + dead_epic + dead_common
- mythic_elf: elf_legendary + elf_epic + elf_rare
- mythic_bear: monster_legendary + monster_epic + monster_rare
- mythic_robot: machine_legendary + machine_epic + machine_common
- mythic_clock: elf_legendary + human_epic + dead_rare
- mythic_mimic: dead_legendary + machine_epic + monster_common
- mythic_swordsman: human_legendary + elf_epic + human_rare
