# Boss skill: double bite

At 100 mana, the enemy Boss plays its bite twice in sequence. Each snap deals one normal AttackPower hit, using skill damage (basicAttack=false), so neither hit generates mana. Ordinary landed basic attacks still grant 20 mana; receiving damage grants Boss no mana. Legendary mana rules are unchanged.

Each bite has its own animation and impact frame. Mana is consumed once. Casting blocks ordinary attacks. The Boss must be in melee range to cast; an invalid target can be replaced by another living opponent already in melee range. Death, wave termination or loss of all nearby targets cancels remaining hits. No ranged or area damage is added.

Reuses the approved generated toothless Boss bite art. No new image generation needed.
