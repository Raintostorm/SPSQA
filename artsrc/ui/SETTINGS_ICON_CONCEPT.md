# Settings pictogram concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Original mobile chibi strategy-game UI, using SpinSquad only as a reference for large readable pictograms and compact hierarchy, not copied assets.

Generate one transparent asset sheet with six separate chunky rounded-square pictogram buttons in 2 rows by 3 columns. Row one: golden gear on blue button; cream speaker with two simple sound waves on blue button; cream mobile phone with two vibration marks on blue button. Row two: cream right-facing play triangle on orange-gold button; cream circular restart arrow on blue button; cream small camp tent with pennant on blue button, representing Home. Thick pure-black uniform outlines, clean flat hard-edged colour bands, 3–5 colours per button. Cheerful chibi proportions. Very large icons readable at 48px. Slight lower flat dark band to indicate pressed depth, no realistic material. Same square silhouette and corner radius on all buttons. Wide transparent gaps, complete unclipped art and generous margins. True antialiased transparent alpha. No text, letters, numbers, gradients, texture, rivets, metal framing, glitter, glow, ground shadow or checkerboard. This is a concept preview, not yet imported into Unity.
