# Original Bandit trial

Built-in imagegen, original art (not APK sprites). Six drawings; animation transforms authored separately. Not imported into Unity.

Use case: stylized-concept. Original sprite poses for ONE tiny chibi rogue/bandit for a casual mobile game. ONE transparent sheet, SIX complete separate figures in TWO rows of THREE, reading order: neutral idle, walk passing pose, attack wind-up; attack strike, attack recovery, upright hurt/death reaction. Same original character in all six: oversized round cream head, a plain ORANGE BANDANA tied at the back with one short tail, dark TEAL short tunic, short dark brown trousers and small rounded brown boots. Face has exactly two black dot eyes, no nose and no other detail. A short broad SILVER curved dagger with a brown grip is held ONE-HANDED in the anatomical RIGHT hand, the NEAR-SIDE arm visible in front of the torso. The LEFT hand always EMPTY. No shield, no second weapon. All figures face RIGHT in the EXACT SAME three-quarter side view. Never flip the body, never swap hands. Make the right-hand wrist grip unambiguous and visible in every pose: the dagger handle must touch the same near-side right hand. Cute squat toy-like proportions, head about 40% total standing height, torso simple, short thick arms/legs. Only large flat solid color shapes, very thick uniform PURE BLACK outline about 6% of figure height, at most 6 flat colors, no extra detail. No gradients, shine, shading, texture, fur, ornament, belts, buckles, fingers, facial complexity. Designed to remain clear at 70px tall. Pose 1 IDLE: neutral stance, near-side right hand and dagger down-forward at hip, left hand empty by torso. Pose 2 WALK: one foot lifted in a small step, same weapon hand retained, torso same scale. Pose 3 WIND-UP: right hand lifts dagger back ABOVE and behind the head, blade points upward/backward; off-hand empty in front for balance. Pose 4 STRIKE: right hand swings forward toward the RIGHT, blade projects horizontally forward at chest level, left hand stays empty tucked near body. Pose 5 RECOVERY: right hand holds dagger low toward the RIGHT after downward slash, body settles back to idle. Pose 6 HURT: STILL UPRIGHT with bent knees and tilted head, eyes two short closed strokes, right hand still gripping dagger low at hip; do NOT draw an already horizontal corpse, animation code will supply the fall. These are logical phases, NOT disconnected character redesigns. Identical physical head/body scale, outfit and camera in all poses. Baseline for boots identical across the standing poses. Allow weapon to extend freely in each direction; no forced square cells or cropping. All figures COMPLETE, all hands, feet, dagger and bandana tail visible. At least 100px transparent gaps around each entire silhouette, generous transparent outer margins. No grid, no cell borders, no text, numbers or labels. TRUE transparent alpha background, antialiased edges; no magenta, white, black background or checkerboard. No ground shadows, no FX, no background. Output a landscape sheet approximately 1800x1200.
