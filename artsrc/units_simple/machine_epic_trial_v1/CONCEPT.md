# MACHINE Epic — simple trial V1

Furnace Crawler: cute compact tracked red-orange furnace machine, round dark grey front furnace opening with flat orange centre, one short dark grey cylindrical forward tool/barrel on RIGHT, a small rear chimney, brown/dark tracks. Preserve old unit concept but remove hoses, rivets, tiny panels and texture. No human arms. Idle level; walk track stance changed; anticipation chassis crouches and front tool retracts; strike front tool thrusts forward for a short-range mechanical ram, chassis lunges RIGHT, not shooting a rifle; recovery tool retracts; hurt chassis slumps down tool still attached same front side. Same chimney, body and front tool every pose. No sparks or attack effect painted in sheet.

Style: approved Bandit proportions, blank humanoid faces, thick pure-black outlines, flat colors, complete silhouettes and original antialiased alpha. Equipment ownership is invariant; no body mirroring. Six authored key poses plus modest procedural motion, not six/eight fully hand-drawn animation clips.

Profile: machine. Demo includes idle, walk, attack and dead. Ground pivots and one common scale; no fixed square crops. Effects separate from sprite art, contact/strike only. Demo-only: Unity assets remain unchanged pending user approval.

Reproduce: python3 tools/preview_simple_bandit.py --directory artsrc/units_simple/machine_epic_trial_v1 --profile machine --effects --expressive --strong-fx
