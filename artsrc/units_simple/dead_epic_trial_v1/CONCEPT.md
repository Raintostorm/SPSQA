# DEAD Epic — simple trial V1

Haunted Tank: cute squat dark purple tracked ghost tank, simple cream skull plaque on turret with two black holes, small cyan flame attached to turret, one short cream/grey cannon barrel pointing RIGHT and two dark track bands. No humanoid arms. Simplify all rivets and armor texture away. Idle turret level, walk track stance subtly changed, anticipation turret retracts and chassis compresses backward, firing pose cannon recoils along SAME horizontal axis still pointing right and chassis rocks, recovery returns barrel, hurt chassis slumped with turret tilted a little downward. Same turret, skull and barrel assembly every pose. No projectile or muzzle flash drawn on sheet.

Style: approved Bandit proportions, blank humanoid faces, thick pure-black outlines, flat colors, complete silhouettes and original antialiased alpha. Equipment ownership is invariant; no body mirroring. Six authored key poses plus modest procedural motion, not six/eight fully hand-drawn animation clips.

Profile: haunted. Demo includes idle, walk, attack and dead. Ground pivots and one common scale; no fixed square crops. Effects separate from sprite art, contact/strike only. Demo-only: Unity assets remain unchanged pending user approval.

Reproduce: python3 tools/preview_simple_bandit.py --directory artsrc/units_simple/dead_epic_trial_v1 --profile haunted --effects --expressive --strong-fx
